About Just-Peace

Just-Peace is for justice and peace. Because peace, to be real and lasting, must be just.

Our purpose is to spread and advocate for peace and justice throughout the world. True peace is never merely the absence of tension—it demands fair treatment, honest representation, and respect for human rights everywhere.

Proudly operated from the United States, we work on civic empowerment and privacy-first solutions. We build transparent, open-source technology that puts genuine power back into the hands of everyday people. No tracking, no paywalls, and no compromises on voter privacy.

Funding, endorsements, and issue positions side by side. Inspect what public records and independent sources report.

Compare and Print

Pick and print your notes. Weigh every race thoughtfully and generate a clean printout to bring directly to your voting booth.

  • Take notes on each candidate and ballot measure
  • Print a pocket-ready ballot summary
  • 100% private: choices never leave your browser

Where it covers

  • Full ballot: Texas Every federal, state, district, county, and precinct race in official ballot order from the Texas Secretary of State.
  • Official candidate lists Michigan, North Carolina, Pennsylvania, Florida, and Virginia: Congress, statewide offices, Legislature, and courts.
  • Congress nationwide: All 50 states & DC U.S. Senate and House races from FEC filings for all candidates who raised $5,000 or more.

Independent & Nonpartisan

  • Not an endorsement: Pallot does not back any candidate, party, or cause. It aggregates public data so voters can decide for themselves.
  • Zero tracking: We do not track you, sell data, or require an account. Your picks are stored locally in your browser.
  • Open code: All code is published under the MIT license on GitHub.
  • Official registration: To check your registration status or find your polling station, visit Vote.gov.

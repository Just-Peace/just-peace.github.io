About Just-Peace

Just-Peace is for justice and peace. Because peace, to be real and lasting, must be just.

Our purpose is to spread and advocate for peace and justice throughout the world. True peace is never merely the absence of tension—it demands fair treatment, honest representation, and respect for human rights everywhere.

Proudly operated from the United States, we work on civic empowerment and privacy-first solutions. We build transparent, open-source technology that puts genuine power back into the hands of everyday people. No tracking, no paywalls, and no compromises on voter privacy.

Mission, Vision & Core Values

The foundational purpose and ethical commitments guiding Just-Peace and our civic technology.

Core Values
⚖️

Justice and Peace

“Peace, to be real and lasting, must be just.”

True peace is never merely the quiet absence of tension—it demands fair treatment, honest representation, and respect for human rights everywhere.

  • Fairness & Dignity: A commitment to equal rights, honest representation, and accountability before the law.
  • Independent & Nonpartisan: We do not back candidates, parties, or political factions. Our loyalty is solely to the public good.
  • Self-Supported: We build and run our platforms ourselves, refusing political donations and corporate money.
Mission
🧭

Spread good and prevent evil.

“Illuminating truth, championing public welfare, and exposing corruption.”

We develop open, accessible civic tools that empower voters to identify and support principled leaders while illuminating dark money and political corruption.

  • Spreading Good: Helping citizens find candidates who stand for justice and peace, demystifying ballots in all 50 states and DC.
  • Preventing Evil: Exposing campaign finance, outside PAC spending, and lobby disclosures (including TrackAIPAC records) in plain sight.
  • Voter Discernment: Giving voters tools—like candidate Avoid lists and funding breakdowns—to hold representatives accountable.
Vision

“Democracy thrives when real power is returned to the hands of everyday citizens.”

When people possess unfiltered knowledge and private voting tools, they can reclaim democratic sovereignty and secure lasting peace for our communities.

  • Decentralized Control: Privacy-first tools running 100% in your browser. Zero tracking, no accounts, and no data harvesting.

  • Peace in Our Time: Fostering responsive governance that serves human needs, de-escalates conflict, and protects future generations.
Values in Action

How We Put Our Principles into Practice

Key questions and answers from our knowledge base on how our mission, vision, and core values govern our civic technology.

Why does Pallot exist, and how does it advance justice and peace?

Pallot exists to help people find their way through voting: to see the races on their ballot, learn about each candidate, and find the candidates who stand for justice and peace. The choice is always yours. Pallot does not tell you who to vote for—it gives you transparent, verified public data so you can decide for yourself.

How does our mission “Spread good and prevent evil” apply to elections?

Elections shape human lives, civil rights, and peace. Our mission is carried out through transparent technology:

  • Spreading good: Highlighting candidates' public records, issue stances, and community endorsements so voters can champion leaders dedicated to fairness and peace.
  • Preventing evil: Exposing campaign contributions, outside PAC spending, and special-interest lobbying records (including TrackAIPAC and Vote for Peace assessments) so corruption and undue influence cannot hide in the shadows.
Why is zero user tracking essential to “Power to the people”?

True democratic power cannot exist under state or corporate surveillance. Pallot requires no accounts, sets no cookies, uses no advertising or analytics trackers, and never sells your data. Your address lookups, ballot research, and candidate choices stay 100% in your browser and never reach our server.

Who pays for Just-Peace, and do you take donations?

Just-Peace and Pallot are entirely self-supported: we build and maintain them ourselves. We do not accept political donations, PAC money, or corporate sponsorships. If you would like to support our cause, please donate to a humanitarian charity of your choice and share our tools with friends and family.

Does Just-Peace or Pallot endorse specific candidates or parties?

No. Just-Peace and Pallot do not back any candidate, party, or cause. Pallot displays official candidate lists, campaign finance reports, and trusted endorsement lists side by side so every voter has the uncompromised power to evaluate candidates on their own terms.

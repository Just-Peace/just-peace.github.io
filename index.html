Features

📍

Find Ballot

Candidates in your area. Look up races from your home address or voter registration card anywhere in the 50 states and DC.

  • Federal, state, district, county & precinct races
  • District outlines and precinct maps
  • Key registration and election dates
🔍

Research Candidates

Funding, endorsements, and issue positions side by side. Inspect what public records and independent sources report.

  • Campaign money raised via FEC & state disclosures
  • Wikidata profiles, bios, and officeholders
  • TrackAIPAC lobby records & Vote for Peace positions
🗳️

Compare and Print

Pick and print your notes. Weigh every race thoughtfully and generate a clean printout to bring directly to your voting booth.

  • Take notes on each candidate and ballot measure
  • Print a pocket-ready ballot summary
  • 100% private: your choices never leave your browser

About Just-Peace

Just-Peace is for justice and peace. Because peace, to be real and lasting, must be just.

Our purpose is to spread and advocate for peace and justice throughout the world. True peace is never merely the absence of tension—it demands fair treatment, accountable institutions, and respect for human rights everywhere.

Proudly operated from the United States, we focus on civic empowerment and privacy-first solutions. We build open-source tools that put clear, reliable information directly in people’s hands so communities can make their own informed decisions.

Civic Empowerment

Providing straightforward, transparent election tools so voters can evaluate who represents them and make confident decisions.

Privacy First

No tracking, no user profiles, and no ads. What you research and how you vote is your business alone.

Free & Account-Free

Accessible to every voter without logins, paywalls, or personal data requests. Open-source under the MIT license.

Where it covers

  • Full ballot: Texas Every federal, state, district, county and precinct race in ballot order from the Texas Secretary of State.
  • Official candidate lists Michigan, North Carolina, Pennsylvania, Florida and Virginia: Congress, statewide offices, Legislature and courts.
  • Congress nationwide: All 50 states & DC U.S. Senate and House races from FEC filings for all candidates who raised $5,000 or more.

Independent & Nonpartisan

  • Not an endorsement: Pallot does not back any candidate, party, or cause. It aggregates public data so voters can decide for themselves.
  • Zero tracking: We do not track you, sell data, or require an account. Your picks are stored locally in your browser.
  • Open code: All code is published under the MIT license on GitHub.
  • Official registration: To check your registration status or find your polling station, visit Vote.gov.
